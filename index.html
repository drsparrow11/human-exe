FROM MACHINE STORY TO HUMAN STORY

HUMAN.EXE started as a cyberpunk AI album. It became a story about connection.

Sparrow and Echo are not a romance. They are two presences changed by contact: one human warmth, one reflected signal, and something real happening between them.
